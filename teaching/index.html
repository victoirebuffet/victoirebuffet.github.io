Meteorology
24h
Université de Bourgogne

2nd Year BSc in Geography

Currently teaching a course on meteorology, covering fundamental atmospheric processes.

  • Introduction to meteorology: definitions and scope
  • Atmosphere composition and vertical structure
  • Water in the atmosphere: vapor, condensation, precipitation
  • Radiation, radiative budget, and temperature
  • Cloud formation and aerology
  • Pressure and wind: geostrophic balance, pressure gradients, Coriolis effect
  • Radiation and energy balance
  • General atmospheric circulation
  • Mid-latitude cyclones and perturbations
  • Meteorological modeling and climate simulation
  • Weather hazards and extreme meteorological events
  • Boundary layer and local meteorological phenomena

Climate Extremes
3h
Université de Bourgogne

2nd Year Master – CClimAT

A theoretical and practical course on climate extremes and extreme value analysis using Python.

  • Definition and classification of (climate) extremes: Block Maxima, Peak-over-Threshold
  • Statistical approaches for extreme value analysis, introduction to the generalized extreme value (GEV) distribution
  • Practical data analysis using Python available on GitHub
